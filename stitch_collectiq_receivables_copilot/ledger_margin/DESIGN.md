## Brand & Style

This design system structures the interaction landscape for receivables management, reconciliation workflows, and payment operations. It treats financial operations not as transactional combat, but as an ongoing audit-grade relationship between counter-parties. The visual character merges the quiet precision of classical accounting stationery with the high-throughput clarity of modern enterprise software.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Intention
- **Disciplined Precision:** Every figure, balance, and invoice status appears deliberate, anchored, and immutable. 
- **Institutional Calm:** In contentious scenarios—such as overdue payments, disputed deductions, or collection workflows—the visual architecture lowers cognitive friction and emotional heat. The interface operates with the neutrality of an impartial auditor.
- **Auditable Transparency:** AI-assisted extractions, automated payment projections, and natural language match suggestions are cleanly distinguished from settled ledger truth through distinct structural affordances.

### Design Movement: Classical Ledger Modernism
The visual system builds upon modern institutional restraint:
- **Paper & Ink Foundation:** Warm off-white canvas tones replace sterile cold greys, giving long operational sessions the comfort of high-grade ledger sheets.
- **Rule Lines Over Shadows:** Elevation relies primarily on structured hairline rules and tonal surface nesting rather than dramatic blurs or floating depths.
- **Data-Dense Quietude:** High informational density is sustained through proportional margins, tabular typography, and calibrated semantic status tokens.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for high-volume enterprise financial oversight. It balances a calming, ink-derived primary teal (`#0E5A6B`) with warm unbleached paper foundations (`#F6F4EF`) and high-contrast structural typography (`#1B1F27`).

### Core Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#F6F4EF`):** Applied to the outer viewports, canvas backdrops, and navigation gutters. Provides visual rest and reduces eye strain across multi-hour operational shifts.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for actionable modules, ledgers, invoice tables, inspection sheets, and active focus panels.
- **Primary Teal (`#0E5A6B`):** Establishes institutional authority. Applied to primary CTAs, active selection states, navigational keystones, and validated relationship linkages.
- **Neutral Boundaries (`#DEDCD5` / `#E3DFD6`):** 1px structural hairline rules separating accounts, line items, and totals without cluttering the screen.

### Status System & Financial Taxonomy
Status indicators are strictly paired: low-saturation tinted backgrounds are combined with high-contrast text and structural accent borders to guarantee WCAG AAA legibility.
- **Verified / Settled (`#14532D` text on `#E3F3E8` with `#1E7A45` accent):** Signals matched payments, cleared funds, and validated invoices.
- **Uncertain / Attention (`#7A2E00` text on `#FFEBD6` with `#C2570C` accent):** Signals pending approval, remittance discrepancy, or unallocated balance.
- **Dispute / Critical (`#8A1C2B` text on `#FBE5E8` with `#B42339` accent):** Signals active counter-party contestations, chargebacks, or past-due thresholds.
- **AI Interpretation / Copilot Extraction (`#3B3280` text on `#EDEBF7` with dashed `#6B63A8` boundary):** Denotes machine-suggested remittance allocations, parsed unstructured emails, or automated likelihood scores.
- **Paused / Holding (`#6B4A00` text on `#FFF2C7` with `#A06A00` accent):** Signals deliberate dunning pauses, forbearance agreements, or grace periods.
- **Neutral / Draft (`#2F3947` text on `#ECEFF3`):** Inactive counter-parties, draft collection cadences, or archival logs.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is built on IBM Plex Sans for systematic interface operations, paired with IBM Plex Mono for numerical accuracy and machine-generated data.

### Structural Rules
- **Monospace Alignment for Balances:** Any currency figure, aging bracket, invoice number, transaction ID, or reconciliation delta must render using the `numeral-*` roles (IBM Plex Mono). This prevents layout shifts across dynamic data updates and aligns tabular decimal points.
- **Labels & Column Headers:** Micro-labels and table headers use uppercase `label-md` or `label-sm` with letter spacing (`0.04em` to `0.05em`) in muted ink (`#505968`), emulating physical ledger balance headers.
- **Body Copy Restraint:** Running text never drops below 13px (`body-sm`). Customer communications, dunning copy reviews, and dispute summaries use `body-md` at 14px with a 20px line-height for rapid scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around the "Ledger & Margin" mental model: clean, uncrowded structural margins enclose dense, disciplined work surfaces.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Split Work Surface:** On desktop displays (1280px+), the interface runs a fixed-flexible hybrid layout: a 280px navigation rail, a flexible primary ledger grid (minimum 720px), and an optional 420px contextual inspector/copilot drawer.
- **Table Grid Rhythm:** Data grids do not float arbitrarily. Rows feature a standardized vertical padding (`space-sm` for compact density, `space-md` for standard views). Column gaps adhere strictly to `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Full 12-column ledger view with simultaneous ledger summary, line items, and contextual assistant drawer.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. The assistant drawer docks beneath or shifts into an overlay sheet. Outer margins tighten to `margin-tablet` (1.5rem).
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked stream. Account balances, status chips, and action bars adapt to sticky anchors. Outer margins reduce to `margin` (1rem).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows and glowing neons, using ink-and-paper architectural separation instead.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Tone `#F6F4EF`. No elevation, no border. The base paper upon which operational ledgers sit.
- **Level 1 (Card / Ledger Panel):** Tone `#FFFFFF`. Bound by a 1px solid hairline border (`#DEDCD5`). Shadow is ambient and subtle: `0px 1px 3px rgba(27, 31, 39, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Active Selectors, Filter Menus):** Tone `#FFFFFF`. Bound by a 1px solid border (`#DEDCD5`) with a focused drop shadow: `0px 4px 12px rgba(27, 31, 39, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs, Payment Verification Overlays):** Tone `#FFFFFF`. Bound by a 1px border (`#DEDCD5`), supported by a soft backdrop curtain (`rgba(27, 31, 39, 0.35)`) and a defined lift shadow: `0px 12px 32px rgba(27, 31, 39, 0.12)`.
- **Level AI (Copilot Suggestions & Unsettled Match States):** Tone `#EDEBF7`. Visual boundary is a 1px dashed or dotted border (`#6B63A8`), signaling to the operator that the content is an algorithmic draft rather than confirmed ledger truth.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a disciplined geometric standard centered on an 8px radius (`roundedness: 2`).

### Radius Tokens
- **Standard (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for cards, tables, panels, modals, buttons, and text inputs. Balances contemporary software polish with the rectilinear structure of a ledger.
- **Sub-element (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used for internal table selections, checkboxes, small status chips, and mono code badges.
- **Large (`1rem` / `16px`):** Reserved for outer app shells, major dashboard view containers, or sliding side sheets.
- **Pill / Circular (Full Radius):** Restricted to unread notification badges, avatar initial rings, and numerical quantity tags. Primary action buttons, tabs, and cards **must never** be pill-shaped.

### Shape-Coded Status Dimensions
To maintain visual clarity beyond color alone:
- **Verified Items:** Symmetrical rounded rectangles (4px corner) with solid left edge border accents (3px width).
- **Disputed Items:** Angular corner tags or sharp indicators that interrupt the standard rhythm.
- **AI Suggested Rows:** Border styling shifts from continuous hairline to a distinct 1.5px dashed pattern (`stroke-dasharray: 4 3`).
