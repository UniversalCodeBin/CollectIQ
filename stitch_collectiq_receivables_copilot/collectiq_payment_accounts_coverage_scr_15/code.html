Why Coverage Matters

SAD AC-15 & Gate G4 Spec Deterministic Rule

CollectIQ only treats an invoice as “outstanding with current evidence” when receiving accounts are covered.

When statement gap is greater than 24 hours, Gate G4 suppresses automated reminder dispatch. If a customer paid yesterday via NEFT/RTGS, chasing them today creates customer relationship damage.
